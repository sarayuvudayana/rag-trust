COLLEGE ACADEMIC & CAMPUS POLICY HANDBOOK (2026-2027)

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY
1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement:
All undergraduate and postgraduate students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the End-Semester Final Examinations.
1.2 Medical Leave and Condonation:
Students with medical emergencies or representing the college in official sports or academic events can be granted condonation up to 10%, reducing the requirement to 65%, provided official medical certificates or duty leaves are submitted within 5 working days of absence.
1.3 Detention Rule:
Any student whose attendance falls below 65% under any circumstances will be strictly detained and will not be permitted to write the final examination. They must repeat the course in the subsequent academic year.

SECTION 2: LIBRARY TIMINGS & BORROWING RULES
2.1 Operational Hours:
The Central Library is open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. On Sundays and official public holidays, the library operates on reduced hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
2.2 Book Lending Limits:
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 3 books simultaneously for a duration of 14 days. Postgraduate and PhD scholars may borrow up to 6 books for a duration of 30 days.
2.3 Overdue Fines:
A late return fine of $1.00 per day per book will be charged for the first 7 days of delay. After 7 days, the fine increases to $2.50 per day per book until returned.

SECTION 3: GRADING SYSTEM & PASSING CRITERIA
3.1 Evaluation Breakdown:
The evaluation consists of 40% Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) including quizzes, assignments, and mid-term exams, and 60% End-Semester Examination (ESE).
3.2 Minimum Passing Marks:
To pass a subject, a student must score at least 40% in the End-Semester Examination and an aggregate of 50% across both CIA and ESE combined.
3.3 Letter Grades:
Marks 90-100%: Grade 'O' (Outstanding, Grade Point 10)
Marks 80-89%: Grade 'A+' (Excellent, Grade Point 9)
Marks 70-79%: Grade 'A' (Very Good, Grade Point 8)
Marks 60-69%: Grade 'B+' (Good, Grade Point 7)
Marks 50-59%: Grade 'B' (Above Average, Grade Point 6)
Marks below 50%: Grade 'F' (Fail, Grade Point 0)

SECTION 4: HOSTEL AND CURFEW RULES
4.1 In-Time Curfew:
All resident students must return to their respective hostel premises before 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends.
4.2 Night-Out Permissions:
Students intending to stay out overnight must submit a digital leave application signed and approved by their parents at least 24 hours in advance to the hostel warden.
